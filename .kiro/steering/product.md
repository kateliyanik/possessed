# Product: POSSESSED

POSSESSED is a Skeleton Crew entry for Kiroween 2026. It is a lean template (PossessionKit) that turns ordinary phones into one collective interface for a shared screen. People scan a QR code; there is no install and no account.

Two applications are built on the template, each in its own folder:

- `apps/spirit-board/`: the room's combined tilt moves one planchette across an original spirit board.
- `apps/possessed-talk/`: an audience steers a presentation with tilt votes, confidence scales and anonymous ghost reactions.

## Rules that shape every decision

- The demo moment is a room of real people moving one thing together. Favour responsiveness and reliability over features.
- Never use the trademark "Ouija" in names, UI text, code identifiers, assets or the video. Say "spirit board".
- All audio and art must be original or properly licensed. No third-party logos.
- Every motion control must have a touch alternative.
- The spooky design must show real system state (who is connected, balance of influence, reconnects), not just decorate.
- One phone has one vote. No device may gain influence by sending more messages or reconnecting.
